Three receipts, three situations

  • Payment receipt

    A customer pays an invoice in full or in part. The receipt names the invoice and shows the balance, so both ledgers agree.

  • Rent receipt

    A tenant pays the month’s rent. The landlord signs a receipt naming the property and the month covered.

  • Advance or deposit

    Money is taken before delivery: a booking amount, a tailoring or furniture order, an event, a security deposit.

What each field in the receipt format records

A receipt answers five questions: who paid, how much, when, how and for what.

Receipt no.
A running number from your receipt book or file. Keep it separate from your invoice series.
Date
The day the money reached you. For a cheque, this is the day you were handed the cheque, with a note that it is subject to realisation.
Received from
The payer’s name. If someone pays on behalf of a customer, write both names.
Amount and amount in words
The figure and the same amount written out. The words make the figure much harder to alter afterwards.
Payment mode
Cash, UPI, card, cheque, NEFT, RTGS or IMPS.
Reference
The UPI reference, UTR number or cheque number and bank. It lets either side find the entry in a bank statement.
Towards
What the payment is for: an invoice number, a month’s rent, an advance for a named order.
Balance
What remains due after this payment. Write “Nil” when the account is settled.
Received by
The name and signature of the person who took the money, with the shop stamp if you use one.

Receipt and invoice: how they differ

PointInvoiceReceipt
What it provesGoods or services were sold and an amount is dueMoney was received
When it is issuedAt the time of sale or supplyWhen payment arrives
Main contentsItems, rates, tax and totalPayer, amount, mode and purpose
Who needs itThe buyer, to know what to payThe payer, as proof of payment
One sale can haveOne invoiceSeveral receipts, one per instalment

A counter bill marked “Paid” does both jobs for a cash sale. A separate receipt matters when payment comes later or in parts. For the bill itself, see the invoice formats.

Points to settle before you sign

Revenue stamp. A one-rupee revenue stamp is commonly affixed and signed across on receipts for cash above ₹5,000. Practice and stamp rules differ between states and by mode of payment, so confirm what applies locally.

Advances under GST. A GST-registered business that takes an advance is expected to issue a receipt voucher with details set out in the GST rules, and the tax treatment of advances is not the same for goods and for services. Ask your chartered accountant how it applies to you.

Part payments. Give a new receipt for every instalment and carry the balance forward on each one. If dues are piling up, read how to collect pending payments from customers.

  • Never issue a receipt before the money is in hand or in the bank.
  • Keep a copy of every receipt, in a duplicate book or as a saved file.
  • Do not overwrite an amount. Cancel the receipt and write a new one.

Receipt questions

Is a payment receipt format different from a money receipt format?

No. Both names are used for the same document: a signed acknowledgement that a stated amount was received. “Money receipt” is the older term and is still widely used.

Is a revenue stamp needed on a receipt?

A revenue stamp is commonly affixed on cash receipts above ₹5,000. The requirement can differ by state and by how the payment was made, so confirm locally or with your accountant.

Can I give a receipt for a UPI or bank transfer?

Yes. Write the mode and the transaction reference on the receipt. Check that the money shows in your account before you sign.

Does a rent receipt need the landlord’s PAN?

The receipt itself has no fixed legal layout. Employers often ask salaried tenants for the landlord’s PAN when the rent is above a limit set by the income tax rules, so many landlords print it. The tenant should check the current limit with their employer or tax adviser.

Can I download these receipts in Word and Excel?

Yes. Each of the three layouts downloads as a Word document (.docx) and an Excel workbook (.xlsx), with sample values or blank.

What if I make a mistake on a receipt I have already given?

Take back the original if you can, mark both copies “Cancelled” and issue a fresh receipt with a new number. Keep the cancelled copies in your file.
