Whether you stock fifty items or fifty thousand, you always know what is on the shelf and what to reorder.

  • Low-stock alerts

The stock cycle

POS inventory management software that follows every bill

Stock is never typed in twice. It moves because your buying and selling moves.

  1. 1

    Add your items

Variants and units

Retail inventory management software that understands sizes, colours and boxes

Create the item once and add its variants. Sell by the piece, buy by the box, and LocalPOS keeps both counts in step.

  • See at a glance which size or colour has run out.

What is inventory management software?
Inventory management software tracks the items you hold, how many you have and how they move in and out. It replaces stock registers and spreadsheets with live counts that update as you buy and sell.
Is LocalPOS suitable as retail inventory management software?
Yes. Retail shops need quick item lookup, variants, units, low-stock alerts and stock that follows every bill. LocalPOS provides all of these in the same system you use to bill.
Does this work as POS inventory management software?
It does. Because billing and stock live together, a sale at the counter reduces stock immediately. There is no separate register to update.
How do low-stock alerts work?
You set a minimum quantity for an item. When stock falls to that level, the item shows up in your low-stock list so you can reorder before it runs out.
Can I add many items at once?
Yes. You can add items in bulk instead of typing each one separately, which saves hours when you first move your stock into LocalPOS.
