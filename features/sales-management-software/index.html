Sales management software for small business owners

LocalPOS is sales management software that keeps every sale, quotation and return in one place. Create an invoice in seconds, turn a quotation into a bill with a single click, and see who bought what and when. Small teams get the clarity of a large company without the paperwork.

What you get

Sales management software that follows each sale from quote to payment

Every step of a sale is linked to the one before it, so nothing is typed twice and nothing gets lost.

  • Quotations that become invoices

    Send a quote, and when the customer agrees, turn it into an invoice without typing it again.

  • Returns and credit notes

    Record a return against the original bill. Stock and the customer’s balance adjust together.

  • Pricing and discount control

    Keep price lists for retail and wholesale customers, and limit who can change a price.

  • A daily sales register

    Every bill of the day in one list, with totals by payment mode.

  • Sales by item, customer and staff

    Find out what sells, who buys and which team member is closing the most sales.

  • Retail and wholesale

    Bill a single walk-in customer or a large order from a trade buyer, using the same screen.

Daily register

Sales management software for small business, with every bill in one list

Open the register and see the day: each invoice, who it was for, how it was paid and what is still due.

  • Filter by date, customer, staff or payment mode

  • Open any bill to reprint, share or return it

What is sales management software?
Sales management software records and organises your sales: quotations, orders, invoices, returns and payments. It shows what you sold, to whom and for how much, so you can run the business on facts.
Is there sales management software free to use?
Free tools usually limit invoices, users or features. You can book a free LocalPOS demo to see the full flow with your own products before you choose a plan. Plan details are on our pricing page.
Is LocalPOS suitable sales management software for small business?
Yes. It is built for owners and small teams, with simple screens, quick invoicing and clear reports, and no training manual needed to get started.
Is it retail sales management software?
It works for retail counters and for wholesale or trade sales. You can bill walk-in customers quickly and manage larger orders with quotations and price lists.
Can I turn a quotation into an invoice?
Yes. Items, prices and tax carry over to the invoice, so you do not enter anything twice.
