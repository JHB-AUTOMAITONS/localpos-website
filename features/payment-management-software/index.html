What is payment management software?
Payment management software records the money your business receives and pays: customer payments, part payments, advances and supplier payments. It shows which invoices are paid and which are still due.
Can I record part payments?
Yes. Accept any amount against an invoice and the rest stays as a balance due. You can add further payments later until it is cleared.
Can I see which customers have pending payments?
Yes. You get a list of customers with dues, grouped by how long they have been pending, so you can prioritise follow-ups.
Does LocalPOS process payments for me?
LocalPOS records payments against your bills. You keep receiving money the way you do today, by cash, UPI or card, and LocalPOS keeps the books straight.
