Barcode billing software that bills as fast as you can scan

LocalPOS is barcode billing software for shops that sell many items quickly. Scan a product and its name, price and tax appear on the bill at once. Print your own barcode labels, scan them at the counter, and stock updates itself with every sale.

What you get

Barcode billing software with scanner support for faster checkout

Typing is where mistakes and queues come from. A scan takes it out of the picture.

  • Scan to bill

    Each scan adds the item to the bill with the right price and tax. No typing, no searching.

  • Works with common scanners

    Use the USB or Bluetooth barcode scanners you can find in any market. If it can scan into a text box, it can scan into a bill.

  • Print your own labels

    Create barcode labels with item name and price for products that do not come with one.

  • Use existing barcodes

    Keep the manufacturer’s barcodes on packaged goods and only label what is missing.

  • Fewer wrong items

    A scanned code picks exactly the right item, so similar-looking products do not get mixed up.

  • Count stock by scanning

    Walk the shelves with a scanner to check quantities, and fix differences with a stock adjustment.

Labels

Print barcode labels for everything that does not come with one

Packaged goods already carry a barcode, so keep it. For loose items, your own packs and repacked goods, print labels from LocalPOS.

  • Choose the items and how many labels you need
  • Include the item name, price and barcode
  • Print, stick and start scanning
How it works

From shelf label to a finished bill

  1. 1

    Give every item a barcode

    Keep the barcode on the pack, or print a label from LocalPOS.

  2. 2

    Scan at the counter

    Each scan adds the item. Scan the same item again to raise the quantity.

  3. 3

    Take payment

    Choose how the customer pays and print or share the bill.

  4. 4

    Stock updates

    The items on the bill leave stock right away.

What you need

Simple hardware you can buy almost anywhere

No special equipment. If your scanner can type into a text box, it can scan into a bill.

  • Barcode scanner

    A USB or Bluetooth scanner that types like a keyboard. Plug it in and scan.

  • Label printer

    Print price labels with a barcode for loose items and your own packs.

  • Receipt printer

    Print the bill the moment payment is taken, if you want paper copies.

  • Your computer or tablet

    The same device you use for billing. No separate handheld needed.

Stock counts

Inventory management software with barcode reader support for quick stock counts

Walk the shelves with your scanner instead of a notebook. Scan each item, compare with the system and fix any difference in a few taps.

  1. 1

    Pick a shelf or category

    Start with one section so counts stay manageable.

  2. 2

    Scan every item

    Scan each pack on the shelf. Repeat scans add up the quantity.

  3. 3

    Compare with the system

    See what you counted next to what LocalPOS expects.

  4. 4

    Adjust the differences

    Accept the count and record a reason for each change.

Questions about barcode billing software

What do I need to start with barcode billing?
You need LocalPOS on a computer or tablet and a barcode scanner. If your products already carry barcodes, you can start scanning straight away. For loose items, print labels from LocalPOS.
Does LocalPOS work as barcode billing software with scanner support?
Yes. LocalPOS works with the common USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners that act like a keyboard, so there is no special driver to hunt for.
Can I print my own barcode labels?
Yes. You can create labels with the item name, price and barcode, and print them on a label printer to stick on products.
Can I count stock with a barcode reader?
Yes. Inventory management software with a barcode reader makes counts quick: scan each item on the shelf, compare the result with the system and adjust any difference.
What if an item has no barcode?
You can still add it to a bill by searching its name, or give it a barcode by printing a label.
