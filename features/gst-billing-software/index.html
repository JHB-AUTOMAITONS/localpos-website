How our GST billing software works out the tax

Tax is where billing mistakes cost real money. LocalPOS keeps the rules in one place so every bill follows them.

  • Correct tax on every line

    Set the GST rate and HSN code once on each item. LocalPOS applies them on every bill.

  • CGST, SGST and IGST sorted

    The tax split follows the place of supply: same-state sales show CGST and SGST, other-state sales show IGST.

  • Tax inclusive or exclusive

    Sell at MRP with tax included, or add tax on top. The invoice shows the taxable value either way.

  • Clean invoice numbering

    Invoices follow a running series, with room for a separate series per store or document type.

  • GST-ready reports

    See tax collected by rate, HSN-wise sales and tax summaries that your accountant can work from.

  • B2B and B2C together

    Add the customer’s GSTIN for business sales, and skip it for walk-in customers.

What is GST billing software?
GST billing software creates invoices with the details GST requires, such as GSTIN, HSN codes, tax rates and the CGST, SGST or IGST split, and keeps records you can use for returns.
Is there GST billing software free to use?
Some tools offer free plans with limits on invoices or features. With LocalPOS you can book a free demo to see how GST invoicing works with your own items and decide from there. Plan details are on our pricing page.
Can I use GST billing software online?
Yes. LocalPOS keeps your invoices online, so you can create bills and check your GST reports from any device with an internet connection.
Does LocalPOS handle CGST, SGST and IGST?
Yes. The tax split follows the place of supply on the invoice. Sales within your state show CGST and SGST; sales to another state show IGST.
Is this Indian GST billing software or a generic tool?
LocalPOS is built for Indian businesses first: GSTIN fields, HSN codes, rupee formatting and the tax splits Indian invoices use are part of the product, not an add-on.
Will it replace my accountant?
No. It keeps your invoices and tax figures organised so your accountant has clean records to work from. Always confirm rates and filing rules with your chartered accountant.
