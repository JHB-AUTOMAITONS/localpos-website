About LocalPOS

Business software that feels as simple as a notebook

LocalPOS exists to give every shop, restaurant and store the tools bigger businesses take for granted, without the complexity that usually comes with them.

Our mission

To make billing and business software simple enough for any owner to use on their first day.

Why we exist

Because most small businesses still run on paper and memory

Walk into almost any shop and you will find the same things: handwritten bills, a stock register updated at night, a notebook of who owes what, and a calculator for tax. It works until the day it does not.

Business software could fix this, but too much of it is built for accountants and IT teams, not for the person standing at the counter. It asks for training, setup and patience that a busy owner does not have.

LocalPOS brings billing, stock, GST, payments and reports into one place and keeps each of them simple. The aim is that you spend your time on customers, not on paperwork.

Product philosophy

How we think about building LocalPOS

  • Simple before powerful

    A feature that needs a manual is a feature that goes unused. We design each screen so a new team member can use it on day one.

  • Built for India, not adapted to it

    Rupees, GSTIN, HSN codes, UPI and the way Indian shops actually bill are part of the product from the start.

  • Everything connected

    A bill updates stock, stock informs purchases, payments settle dues and all of it feeds reports. No double entry.

  • Plain about what it does

    We describe the product in ordinary words and show it working before asking you to commit.

Our values

What we hold ourselves to

  • Respect the owner’s time

    Every extra tap is a cost. We keep the common jobs short.

  • Make the numbers trustworthy

    Totals, tax and stock should be right every time, or the software has failed.

  • Explain, do not impress

    Clear words and honest limits beat clever marketing.

  • Grow with the business

    What works for one counter should still work for ten.
