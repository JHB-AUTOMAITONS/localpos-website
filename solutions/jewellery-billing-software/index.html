Why jewellery billing is different from every other shop

  • One small mistake costs a lot

    A wrong weight or rate on a high-value bill is expensive to fix later.

  • Pieces are hard to track

    With hundreds of unique items, it is difficult to know what is in the showcase and what has sold.

  • Bills have many parts

    Metal value, making charges, stones and tax all need to be shown clearly.

  • Customer history matters

    Jewellery customers return for years and expect you to remember them.

Every piece on record

Best jewellery billing software starts with knowing every piece

Give each piece a tag and keep its full story in one place. Scan the tag at the counter and every detail flows onto the bill.

  • Tag number and description
  • Gross, stone and net weight
  • Purity, such as 22K or 18K
  • Making charge, by weight or fixed
  • Stone details and charges
  • Status: in showcase, sold or with the karigar
