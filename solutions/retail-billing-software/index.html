Where a busy shop loses time and money

Most retail problems are small, repeated many times a day.

  • Queues build up at the counter

    Searching for prices and writing bills by hand slows every customer down.

  • Shelf and system never match

    Stock registers are updated late, so you find out about an empty shelf from a customer.

  • Regulars are strangers

    Without records you cannot see who your best customers are or who owes you money.

  • Daily totals take hours

    Adding up bills, discounts and payment modes at closing time eats your evening.

What is retail billing software?
Retail billing software helps a shop create bills, take payments and keep stock and sales records in one place. It replaces handwritten bills and separate registers with a single system at the counter.
What makes the best retail billing software?
Look for fast billing, reliable stock tracking, clear reports and screens your team can learn in a day. LocalPOS focuses on those basics, and the demo lets you test them with your own products.
Is there free retail billing software?
Free tools are usually limited in invoices, users or features. You can book a free LocalPOS demo to see the full product, and check our pricing page for current plans.
Can LocalPOS work as retail POS software for more than one shop?
Yes. If you grow to several branches, you can manage them from one login with store-wise stock and reports.
Do I need barcode scanners for POS software for retail?
Not to start. You can search items by name. A scanner speeds things up when you sell many items a day, and LocalPOS supports common scanners.
