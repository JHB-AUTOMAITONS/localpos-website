Where service slips between table, kitchen and bill

  • Orders get lost between table and kitchen

    Handwritten slips are misread, and dishes arrive late or wrong.

  • Bills are slow at peak hours

    Guests wait to pay while staff add up items and tax by hand.

  • Table status is unclear

    Nobody knows which tables are free, waiting or ready to pay.

  • Split bills cause arguments

    Sharing a bill between guests means extra maths at the worst moment.

Kitchen workflow

From seating to payment

An order taken at the table is on the kitchen’s printer in the same moment. No running, no shouting, no lost slips.

  1. 1

    Open the table

    Pick a free table and start the order.

  2. 2

    Take the order

    Add dishes with notes like “less spicy” and send them to the kitchen.

  3. 3

    Serve and add more

    Guests order again? Add items to the same table.

  4. 4

    Bill and settle

    Print the bill, split it if needed and record how it was paid.

At the end of the meal

Split the bill without splitting the mood

Guests who dine together rarely pay together. Divide a bill equally, by item, or merge two tables into one, in a few taps.

What is restaurant billing software?
Restaurant billing software manages orders and bills for a food business: tables, items, kitchen tickets, tax and payments. It keeps service quick and your sales records accurate.
Is LocalPOS restaurant POS software?
Yes. It covers table orders, kitchen tickets, bill splitting and daily sales, so it works as restaurant POS software for cafes, restaurants and counters.
What makes the best restaurant billing software?
Speed at the table, clear order flow to the kitchen, correct GST and simple end-of-day reports. A short demo with your own menu is the best way to judge.
Is there restaurant billing software free to use?
Free tools tend to limit features or users. You can book a free LocalPOS demo and check our pricing page for current plans before deciding.
Does it work as restaurant billing software in India with GST?
Yes. Bills carry GST details and show tax correctly, and your sales and tax reports stay ready for your accountant.
