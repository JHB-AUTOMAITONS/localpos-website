The quiet losses in a pharmacy

Most of them come from stock that nobody was watching.

  • Expired stock is a silent loss

    Medicines that expire on the shelf are money you cannot get back.

  • Many batches of one medicine

    The same product arrives in different batches with different expiry dates and prices.

  • Thousands of small items

    Tablets, strips, syrups and devices need quick lookup and precise stock counts.

  • Regulars buy the same things

    Chronic-care customers return monthly, and you want their history at hand.

Medical store billing software built around batches and expiry

Everything is tracked by batch, so expiry stops being a surprise.

What is medical store billing software?
Medical store billing software is billing and stock software designed for pharmacies. It tracks batches and expiry dates, sells in strips or tablets, and keeps customer and tax records.
Is LocalPOS pharmacy billing software?
Yes. It supports batch numbers, expiry tracking, strip and pack units and fast billing, which are the core needs of pharmacy billing software.
What should billing software for medical store owners include?
Batch-wise stock, expiry alerts, quick search and accurate tax. Check that it fits your own workflow with a demo, and follow the rules that apply to your pharmacy.
