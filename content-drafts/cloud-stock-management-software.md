## 2. Keyword cannibalization check

- **Close overlap:** `/blog/cloud-based-inventory-software-benefits/` (18 Nov 2026) targets "cloud based inventory software" and lists "cloud-based stock management system" as a supporting term. Search engines may treat the two queries as the same intent.
- How this post is kept distinct: it is built around stock movement and day-to-day control (six-step workflow, movement tracking, mistakes and fixes, a small-business switch plan), while the earlier post is a benefits list. It links to the earlier post as a companion read.
- Watch both URLs in Search Console for four to six weeks. If they swap positions for the same queries, merge the weaker one into the stronger one with a 301 redirect.
- `/features/inventory-management-software/` owns "inventory management software". This post uses the phrase only as a supporting term and links to that page.
