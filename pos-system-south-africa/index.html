Everyday trading headaches

Whatever you sell, these are the problems that cost owners time at the end of a long day.

  • Takings that do not match

    Cash, card and other payments get mixed together, so reconciling the till drags on.

  • Stocktake surprises

    The count on the shelf and the count in your records disagree, and nobody can say why.

  • Not knowing what actually sells

    Slow lines tie up cash while fast lines run out, because nobody is tracking either.

  • Returns and refunds on scraps of paper

    A return that is not recorded properly leaves stock and the customer’s balance wrong.

What you get

POS software for tracking sales and stock

How LocalPOS helps you see what happens at the till and behind it.

  • A daily sales register

    Every bill of the day in one list, with totals by payment mode.

  • Sales by item, customer and staff

    Find out what sells, who buys and which team member is closing the most sales.

  • Returns that adjust stock

    Record a return against the original bill. Stock and the customer’s balance adjust together.

  • Counts you can explain

    Record damage, loss or a recount with a reason, so every stock change has an answer.

  • Part payments and advances

    Take an advance, accept a part payment and leave the balance due, all against the same invoice.

  • Reports you can share

    Download a report to print, email or hand to whoever reviews the books.

Use cases

What a POS system South Africa businesses need at the till

Four types of business, and what each one usually wants from the software.

  • Retail stores

    A retail POS system should make a bill quick and keep the stock right. LocalPOS searches by name, scans barcodes and updates stock on every sale. See retail billing.

  • Grocery businesses

    Items sold by weight, pack or box can be handled with units, and low-stock alerts flag fast sellers before the shelf is empty. Learn about barcode billing.

  • Restaurants

    Open a table, add to the order as guests ask for more, send it to the kitchen and split the bill. See restaurant billing.

  • Small and medium businesses

    Quote first, then turn the quotation into an invoice without retyping it, and keep retail and wholesale price lists. Read about sales management.

Stock

Inventory management software that keeps counts honest

Stock accuracy comes from recording every movement, not from counting harder. LocalPOS tracks:

  • Sales

    Each bill takes the items out of stock straight away.

  • Purchases

    Goods you receive add to stock, with cost prices updated from the same entry.

  • Adjustments

    Damage, loss and recounts are logged with a reason.

  • Stock value

    See what your stock is worth at cost and at selling price.

What does a POS system do for a retail store?

It creates bills, takes payments and updates stock in one step, then turns your sales into reports so you can see what is selling and what is not.

Can LocalPOS track my stock as well as my sales?

Yes. Stock reduces on each sale and increases when you receive goods. Low-stock alerts, stock adjustments with reasons and a stock value view are part of the inventory feature.

Is LocalPOS suitable for restaurants and grocers?

It has solution pages for restaurants and supermarkets, covering tables and kitchen tickets for restaurants and multi-counter billing for supermarkets.

How do I get started?

Book a free demo, add your items and opening stock, and start billing. A computer or tablet is enough to begin.

Does LocalPOS support South African tax and payments?

That is not confirmed on this site. LocalPOS describes itself as built for Indian businesses first, so ask the team to confirm rand billing, tax and payment types before you decide.
